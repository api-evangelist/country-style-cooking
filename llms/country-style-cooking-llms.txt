# Country Style Cooking (乡村基 / Xiangcunji)

> Country Style Cooking Restaurant Chain is a large Chinese quick-service restaurant group
> founded on 23 November 1996 in Chongqing by Li Hong and Zhang Xingqiang. It operates the
> directly-managed Xiangcunji (乡村基) and Mr. Rice (大米先生) brands, serving Sichuan-style
> fast food cooked to order. The company formerly traded on the NYSE as CCSC. It publishes a
> Chinese-language corporate website only — there is no developer portal, no public API, and
> no machine-readable API artifacts.

## APIs
